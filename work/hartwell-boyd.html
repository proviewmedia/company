A brand that does not look like a template, and an intake system the firm owns instead of a PDF the staff retypes.

The situation

The firm's website came from a legal marketing vendor and looked identical to four other firms in the same county, down to the stock photograph in the hero.

The real cost was operational. New client intake was a PDF emailed out, filled in by hand, scanned crooked, and retyped by a paralegal. Conflict checks happened in a spreadsheet. None of that is a marketing problem, and no web shop had offered to fix it.

What we did

Three disciplines, one engagement.

Brand

A mark and type system built to work on a letterhead, an engagement letter, a business card, and a screen, rather than a logo designed only for a website header.

Website

Organized around the practice areas the firm actually wants more of, with attorney portraits shot in the firm's own offices.

Intake tooling

A client intake and conflict check tool the firm logs into. Structured data in, no retyping, and documents collected before the first meeting rather than during it.
