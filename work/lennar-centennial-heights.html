Lennar, Centennial Heights

Townhomes buyers could walk around before they ever drove out.

A walkthrough film and a navigable 3D aerial capture of the townhomes at Centennial Heights in Centennial Hills, Las Vegas.

The film

The tour, cut for a phone.

Walkthrough film, Centennial Heights Exterior approach, the drive in, and the interior run in one continuous read, so a buyer sees the home the way they would arrive at it.

The 3D capture

Drag it. The whole building is there.

Aerial passes flown around the block and reconstructed into a 3D scene you can orbit in a browser. No app, no plugin, no download.

Interactive 3D scene, drag to orbit Elevation, roofline, setbacks, garage placement, and how the units sit against each other. Questions a floor plan cannot answer and a single photograph will not settle.

The situation

Townhomes are hard to sell on a listing page. Photographs flatten them. A buyer cannot tell how the unit sits in the block, how tall it reads from the street, or what the approach actually looks like, and the model home only answers part of it.

Out of state buyers have the same problem with the drive. Centennial Hills is a thirty minute reality that a map pin does not communicate. Both gaps get solved by showing the place instead of describing it.

What we did

Three disciplines, one visit.

Video production

Shot list built around the buyer's sequence, arrival through interior. Filmed, graded, and cut to a length that survives a phone screen and a short attention span.

Aerial capture

Flown under FAA Part 107 with the airspace cleared against the address before the date was set. Orbits and elevation passes covering the block from every side.

3D reconstruction

The aerial passes reconstructed into a navigable 3D scene that loads in a browser and runs on a phone. Embeddable anywhere a link goes.

Why it matters

One flight, two deliverables.

The same day produces both

The film and the 3D scene come out of one visit and one set of flights, rather than a video crew on one date and a separate capture on another.

It travels

The film runs on the listing, on social, and in paid placements. The 3D scene embeds on a community page or drops into an email as a link that opens on any device.

It answers the questions

Elevation, orientation, spacing between units, where the garage sits. Buyers ask these before they book a visit, and now the answer is a drag of a finger.

Outcome

Lennar left with a walkthrough film and an interactive 3D capture of the Centennial Heights townhomes, both from one production, and both usable on a listing, in an email, or in an ad without further work.
