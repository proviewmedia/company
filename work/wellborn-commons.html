Sample project

A property that needed listing photographs in four days.

Sample project, not delivered client work

Aerial and interior captured in one visit, and a single property site live before the listing went to market.

The situation

The broker had a signed listing agreement and a marketing deadline four days out. The property needed aerials showing the parcel and access, interiors showing the build out, and somewhere to put both.

The alternative was booking a drone operator and a separate interior photographer on separate days, then waiting on a marketing team to assemble a PDF.

What we did

Three disciplines, one engagement.

Aerial

Airspace checked against the address before the date was confirmed, then flown under Part 107. Parcel boundaries, parking, access, and the surrounding corridor.

Property photography

Interiors and ground level exteriors in the same visit, matched in treatment to the aerial so the set reads as one shoot.

Listing site

A single property site with the photography, the specs, and a contact path, live before the listing went to market.
