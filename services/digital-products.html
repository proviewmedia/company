Services

Products taken to a market, not tools built for a team.

Custom software solves an internal problem for a known set of users. A digital product is sold, and it has to survive people who did not ask for it, were not trained on it, and will leave in thirty seconds if it does not make sense.

That is a different job. It needs positioning, onboarding, pricing, and a first run experience that earns the second one.
