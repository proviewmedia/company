Questions

Common questions.

Does it need an app or a headset?

No. It runs in a browser on a phone or a desktop. A headset works if someone has one, but nobody needs one.

How much of the building should we cover?

The spaces that decide the booking. For a venue that is usually the main room, the entry, and one or two supporting spaces. Covering every closet makes the tour worse, not better.

Can this be done with the aerial?

Yes, and it should be. Booked alongside an interactive 3D scene or an aerial capture, the interior and the exterior come from one visit at one mobilization.

Do we need to stage the space?

A tour shows the room as it is, in every direction, so there is nowhere for clutter to hide. We will tell you what to clear before the visit.
