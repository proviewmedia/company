Questions

Common questions.

Do you shoot during production?

Yes. Facility work happens on a normal working day. A shut down plant photographs like a shut down plant.

Can you work around safety requirements?

Yes. Tell us the site rules on the first call. We follow PPE and escort requirements and plan around restricted areas.

Will staff appear in the photographs?

Only with consent. A great deal of facility photography works with nobody identifiable in frame.

How many images do we get?

Scoped per shoot. A facility day typically delivers a set covering a full website plus print, rather than a fixed number.
