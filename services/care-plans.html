Questions

Common questions.

Is a care plan required?

No. You can take full ownership and manage it yourself or with your own team, and we will hand over documentation.

Can we cancel?

Yes. The plan runs a year at a time and does not roll into a longer contract. Whatever we built stays yours either way.

What is not included?

Major new features or a redesign. Those get quoted separately, and we will tell you when a request crosses that line.

How fast is support?

Same business day acknowledgement. Actual turnaround depends on what broke.
