Questions

Common questions.

How long does a website take?

Most marketing sites run four to eight weeks depending on page count and how much content needs writing. Photography, if included, is scheduled inside that.

Do we own it?

Yes. The code, the domain, and every account. There is no license and no hostage situation if you leave.

Can you write the copy?

Yes. We draft it and you approve it. Nothing about your business gets published without your sign off.

What about hosting?

Deployed to a fast static host. We can manage it under a care plan or hand you the keys.
