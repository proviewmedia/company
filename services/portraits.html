Portraits shot where the work happens.

A portrait against a grey studio backdrop tells a viewer nothing except that someone booked a photographer. A portrait in the actual office, plant, or clinic tells them where you work and what the place is like.

Consistent treatment across a team matters more than any single frame. A leadership page where every portrait was shot differently reads as disorganized before anyone has read a name.

Questions

Common questions.

How long does a team session take?

Roughly ten to fifteen minutes per person, scheduled in blocks so nobody is waiting around.

What should people wear?

We send guidance ahead of the session. Generally, what they would wear to meet a client.

Can new hires be added later?

Yes, matched to the existing set so the page stays consistent.

Do you retouch?

Lightly and honestly. Corrected and cleaned. We do not reshape people.
