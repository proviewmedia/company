Questions

How long should a brand film be?

Usually sixty to ninety seconds for a website, with shorter cuts for social. Longer only when there is genuinely more to say.

Do you do aerial video?

Yes, under Part 107, with airspace authorization filed for the location.

Is music licensed?

Yes, and the license is included in delivery. You are not exposed to a takedown.

Can you film interviews?

Yes, recorded on site so people are in their own environment.
