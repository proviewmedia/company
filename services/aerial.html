Questions

Common questions.

Are you licensed?

Yes. FAA Part 107 certified remote pilot. An uncertificated operator flying your property commercially is not legal, and that exposure lands on the property and whoever hired them.

What if the location is in controlled airspace?

Most useful locations are. We check the address and file the required authorization. Some sites need lead time and a small number cannot be flown at all, and we say so before taking a booking.

Do you offer thermal or infrared?

No. We do not carry a thermal sensor. If your scope needs moisture detection, solar fault detection, or heat loss analysis, you need an operator with the right payload.

What about weather?

We reschedule. We will not fly conditions that produce unsafe operations or unusable images, and you do not pay for a visit that produced nothing.
