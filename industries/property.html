Questions

What brokers and builders ask.

How fast can you shoot a listing?

It depends on the address and the airspace over it. Some locations can be scheduled within a couple of days. Locations requiring an authorization on file take longer, and we check before committing to a date.

Are you licensed to fly commercially?

Yes. FAA Part 107 certified remote pilot. Any commercial drone flight legally requires it, and an uncertificated operator creates exposure for the property and whoever hired them.

Do you shoot interiors too?

Yes, in the same visit. Most listings need aerial for context and interiors for detail, and booking them together costs less than two crews on two days.

Can you document a build over time?

Yes. Scheduled visits with repeatable flight paths and fixed angles, so the images stack into a usable record for owners and lenders.

Who owns the photographs?

You do. Full resolution originals, usable across your listings, your site, and your marketing.
