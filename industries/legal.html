Questions

What firms ask.

Do you understand legal advertising rules?

We follow your state bar's advertising requirements and put required disclaimers where they belong. You review all copy before launch, and nothing goes live without the firm's sign off.

Can you write the practice area content?

We draft it and an attorney reviews and approves it. Nothing about your practice gets published without a lawyer in the loop.

Is the intake system something we own?

Yes. It is built for the firm and it is yours. It is not a seat license on somebody else's platform.

Can the intake tool handle confidential information?

We design for it, with access controls and encrypted storage. Specific compliance requirements get scoped on the first call.

Do you photograph attorneys in the office?

Yes. Portraits shot in your actual space read as a real firm. Rented studio backdrops read as stock.
