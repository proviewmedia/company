Questions

What manufacturers ask.

Do you shoot during production?

Yes. Most facility work happens on a normal working day, scheduled around your line. Shutting down for photographs produces images that look like a shutdown.

What about safety and access requirements?

Tell us on the first call. We follow site PPE and escort requirements, and we plan the shoot around restricted areas rather than asking for exceptions.

Can you photograph proprietary equipment without showing the process?

Yes. We agree what is off limits before the shoot and frame around it. You approve every image before anything is published.

Do we own the photographs?

Yes. You get full resolution originals with the right to use them anywhere, including through your distributors and reps.

How long does a facility shoot take?

Usually half a day to a full day on site depending on plant size, plus aerial if the site warrants it.
