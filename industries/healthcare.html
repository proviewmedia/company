Questions

What clinics ask.

Will patients appear in the photographs?

Only with written consent, and only if you want them to. Most clinic photography works without a single patient in frame.

Do you shoot during patient hours?

We schedule around them. Early mornings, closed days, and gaps in the schedule usually cover a full shoot without touching appointments.

Can you handle patient privacy requirements?

We plan the shoot so charts, screens, and identifying information stay out of frame, and you approve every image before publication.

Can intake forms connect to our practice software?

Sometimes directly, sometimes as a structured handoff. It depends on the system you run, and we scope it on the first call.

Do you photograph the team as well as the space?

Yes. Individual portraits and working shots, both shot in your space rather than a rented studio.
