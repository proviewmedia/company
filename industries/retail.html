Questions

What local businesses ask.

We are a small shop. Is this going to be out of our range?

Scope moves with the business. A single location with a straightforward site is a much smaller engagement than a multi site brand, and we tell you the range on the first call rather than after a proposal.

Do you have to close the shop to photograph it?

No. We shoot before opening, after closing, or on a slow day. For most retail spaces the best light is outside business hours anyway.

Can we update the site ourselves afterward?

Yes. Hours, prices, menu items, and photographs are all yours to change, and you get the logins and a short walkthrough at handoff.

We mostly get found on Google and Instagram. Do we even need a website?

You need both, and they feed each other. The Google listing is usually the first thing anyone sees, and it pulls photographs and information that have to come from somewhere. The site is the part you own if a platform changes the rules.

Do you work with trades that have no storefront?

Yes. Plumbers, electricians, landscapers, and contractors get the same treatment, shot on job sites instead of in a shop, plus vehicle lettering if you want it.

Can you do the photography now and the website later?

Often that is the right order. The photographs are useful immediately on your listing and social accounts, and the site gets built around them when you are ready.
