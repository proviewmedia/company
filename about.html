About

A product designer who photographs the businesses he builds for.

Founder portrait, 1200 x 1500

I'm Melvin Morales. I studied at Wentworth Institute of Technology in Boston and have spent my career as a product designer, building software people log into and use to do their jobs.

Proview Studio started in 2017 as the place that work happened outside of a company. Websites, brand systems, and custom software for businesses that needed something built for how they actually operate.

The photography came out of the same problem. Clients needed images their sites could use, and hiring that out meant waiting on somebody else's schedule and accepting whatever came back. Doing both removed the handoff, and it is now the reason to hire the studio at all.

Background

Four industries, from the inside.

Most studios learn an industry from a discovery call. These are sectors I have designed products inside of, which is a different kind of knowing.

Legal

Product design at NetDocuments, in legal document management. Matters, documents, versioning, and the workflows a firm actually runs on, rather than a guess at them from the outside.

Healthcare

Product work in healthcare, where intake, records, and privacy requirements shape every screen. Clinics do not need a brochure. They need the paperwork to stop being paperwork.

Wellness and fitness

Booking, scheduling, membership, and retention. The operational problems behind a practice or a studio, not just the storefront in front of them.

Logistics

Systems where the physical world and the software have to agree. Tracking, status, exceptions, and the reporting that has to survive an audit.
